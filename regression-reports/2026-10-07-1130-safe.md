### TC-07 step 6: "+" quick-add adds every available add-on
- **Expected:** "+" adds Yassai curry as a separate line at 2,900 AMD with no add-ons.
- **Actual:** the line is "Yassai curry · 3,600 AMD · + Extra sticks, Wasabi, Soy Sauce, Marinated Ginger, Sweet Chile, Unagi sauce, Teriyaki sauce". Reproduced on an empty cart after a reload (Subtotal 3,600, Delivery 500, Total 4,100 AMD).
- **Likely cause:** `apps/web/src/components/sections/dish-card.tsx:17` passes the whole `dish` to `addDishToCart`, and `apps/web/src/hooks/useCart.ts:48` adds up every entry in `dish.additions`, so the add-ons the dish *offers* are treated as *selected*. This affects every dish that has add-ons.
